Project Jarvis · Product concept

Collaborative College Planning

Helping students, parents, and counselors build and review an academic plan together.

An early-stage UNC MEITE group project

The planning challenge

Students and families need to connect college goals with choices about courses and preparation. Jarvis explores how a shared plan could support those decisions while keeping students involved as their interests change.

My contribution

I lead product management, UX design, and prototype development for Jarvis, focusing on U.S. students and families. I’ve recruited testers and am supporting testing; once feedback is gathered, I’ll identify themes and prioritize changes to features and workflows. I’m also developing the Business Model Canvas and an approach for reaching our first target market.

Who takes part

  • Students set goals and own their path.
  • Parents review and approve decisions.
  • Counselors guide planning and help validate choices.

Current work and next steps

Introduction

Started in October 2026, Jarvis is an early-stage UNC MEITE group project. The overview and original concept poster make the proposed planning process tangible for discussion and testing. Sample profiles, readiness measures, and progress charts illustrate the concept; they are not validated predictions.

Reflection

Developing Jarvis helped me turn a concern about college readiness into a concept people can examine and test. The design needs to connect goals with practical choices while keeping students in control and involving parents and counselors. Testing and feedback will help show which features and workflows support useful decisions.
