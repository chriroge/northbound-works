UNC MEITE · GROUP PROJECT

College readiness and academic planning

Project Jarvis

Know where you’re going.
Build confidence you’ll get there.

An early-stage UNC MEITE group project

01 From aspirations to a plan

Jarvis explores how families can connect college aspirations to courses, milestones, and experiences while there is still time to prepare.

02 Keep possibilities open

As interests and circumstances change, students would revisit their preparation and consider different pathways with parents and counselors.

Connect today’s choices to tomorrow’s possibilities.
Build a plan that grows with the student.

03 A plan you can keep improving

04 A shared planning process

  • Students set goals and own their path.
  • Parents review and support decisions.
  • Counselors guide planning and help validate choices.

05 Where the concept stands

Start with parent-involved planning before applications. Integration with school planning is a future direction.

An early-stage concept. Poster figures are illustrative, not verified admissions benchmarks or admission probabilities.
