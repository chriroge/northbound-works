Project Jarvis

Know where you’re going.
Build confidence you’ll get there.

An early-stage UNC MEITE group project

Choosing next year’s classes can feel like guessing at a future you can’t yet see.

Students have college aspirations. Parents want to help—but which choices build readiness, and which might close doors?

Project Jarvis is an early-stage UNC MEITE group project exploring how students, parents, and counselors can turn those aspirations into an academic plan they can review, approve, and adjust together.

From Aspirations to Aligned Plans

Jarvis helps students and families turn college aspirations into a clear, actionable academic plan—and keeps it on track.

A Shared Planning Process

  • Students set goals and own their path.
  • Parents review and approve decisions.
  • Counselors guide planning and help validate choices.

Where the Concept Stands

Now: Parent involved academic planning that positions students to preserve options and move towards college goals.

Future: Integration with school planning processes.
