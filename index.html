My Projects & Ideas About Me

Hi, I’m Chris.

I’ve spent more than 25 years helping technology organizations set direction, build products, and change how they work. Through UNC’s MEITE program, I’m now bringing that experience to education.

I’m exploring how universities can adapt as AI changes learning and work, and how more undergraduates can gain meaningful experience before graduation. I’m especially interested in bringing students from different fields together with faculty and outside partners to tackle real problems without predetermined answers.

My starting question is practical: what would it take to make these experiences easier to offer, valuable to everyone involved, and available to more students?

EDUC 701 introduction & reflection

Introduction

This two-page brochure helps students approach college planning earlier and with their own interests, strengths, and needs at the center. Drawing on my experience as a parent of two high school seniors and a conversation with their principal, I explore the limits of counselor capacity and the value of sustained guidance. The brochure pairs a comparison of planning approaches with an Explore–Discover–Prepare–Refine framework and practical steps for students, parents, and counselor collaboration.

Reflection

Developing this artifact helped me connect a personal experience with a broader educational design problem: access to timely, individualized guidance. I moved from thinking mainly about admissions outcomes to considering how exploration and preparation can grow together. It also clarified the role I want to play as a parent—supporting informed choices while keeping the student’s agency and the counselor’s expertise at the center.
