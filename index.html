About Me

I'm an experienced technology and operations leader exploring how thoughtful design, emerging technology, and new models of education can help people learn, grow, and expand what they're capable of doing.

After more than 25 years designing products, experiences, and operating models in technology organizations, I'm bringing that experience into educational innovation through UNC's MEITE program. I'm especially interested in using design thinking, systems thinking, and emerging technologies to understand real human problems and create learning experiences and solutions that meaningfully improve people's capabilities.

I believe learning is more than acquiring information or completing an activity. Learning is a change in what someone is capable of understanding or doing. Great educational experiences should therefore be designed around that change—not simply around the content, technology, or activity used to produce it
