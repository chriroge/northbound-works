About Me

I help teams solve complex problems and turn ideas into practical improvements. For over 25 years, I’ve worked in customer-centered design, product leadership, and project delivery at Accenture, Cisco, and Cisco Meraki.

I’m studying Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship at UNC–Chapel Hill and applying that experience to education.

My approach is to set direction, connect people, remove obstacles, and test ideas in practice. I want to apply this experience in education, public good, and health.
