My Projects & Ideas About Me

Hi, I’m Chris.

I solve problems.

The easy problems have mostly been solved. The ones that remain are messy, complicated, and consequential. They don’t come with clear answers. They involve people, organizations, competing priorities, and systems that don’t always work the way they should. Those are the problems that interest me.

I’ve spent more than 25 years working across strategy, operations, and product management—helping organizations navigate complexity, find direction, and turn ideas into meaningful progress.

Today, I’m bringing that experience to a new set of challenges at the intersection of education, technology, and the future of work.

I’m still learning. Still exploring. Still figuring out what’s next.
