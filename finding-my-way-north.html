Introduction

Finding My Way North is an interactive career map created in October 2026 for my EDUC 701 portfolio. It traces a throughline I can now see across different roles and industries: learning how to introduce consequential change. The settings changed—from supporting a project, to enabling an international market, introducing a new business model, reshaping an organizational paradigm, designing an end-to-end customer journey, and applying AI to sales effectiveness—but the work kept returning to the same challenge: set direction, remove obstacles, and help people move.

Reflection

This artifact matters because it reveals that my career has not been a series of disconnected roles. Each chapter expanded the scale of change I learned to support. The pattern is now clear: execute well, become a bridge, make yes possible, lead transitions, redesign systems, integrate disciplines, and choose consequential problems. That recognition gives Northbound Works its direction.
