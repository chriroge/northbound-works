Introduction

Created in October 2026, this interactive map connects eight career chapters with the challenges, actions, and lessons that shaped my approach. It shows how experience in strategy, operations, and product management informs my work in education and interest in wider social challenges.

Reflection

Building the map helped me see how each role prepared me for the next. The recurring lessons were to earn trust, connect expertise, remove barriers, and test better ways of working. Those lessons now guide the projects I pursue through Northbound Works.
